Privacy Policy — Silver Japanese: Vocabulary

Last Updated: October 2, 2026

Quick summary: Silver Japanese includes AI-generated definitions and example sentences, bundles pronunciation audio generated with LuvVoice, TTSMP3 and TTSMaker, displays personalized ads, and uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to collect and manage consent for users in the EU/EEA and the UK. The app has no account system and no server of ours — we never receive your personal data. It asks for your date of birth once, at onboarding, to confirm you meet the minimum age of 13 and to apply age-appropriate advertising; that date of birth stays on your device and is never transmitted. The app is not directed to children.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how the application "Silver Japanese: Vocabulary" (the "app") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the app. By using the app you agree to the terms described here.

The app is distributed by an individual developer and operates entirely on your device. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server operated by us.

3. How We Use Information

  • Provide and maintain app functionality.
  • Remember language preferences and study progress.
  • Enforce the minimum age of 13.
  • Serve advertisements, including personalized ads, and apply age-appropriate treatment based on your age classification.
  • Improve app stability and the user experience.

Because we operate no server, none of these uses involve us receiving or processing your personal data.

4. Age and Age-Appropriate Advertising

On first launch the app asks for your date of birth. This is used for two purposes only:

  • Minimum age. The app requires a minimum age of 13. If the date of birth entered indicates you are younger than 13, the app will not let you proceed.
  • Advertising treatment. Your date of birth is used to work out an age classification, which is passed to Google so that advertising is treated appropriately for your age group. Two signals are derived:
  • Teen — if you are between 13 and 17 inclusive.
  • Under the age of digital consent — if you are under the age at which your country requires digital consent for advertising (the app uses 16 as the general threshold, and defers to Google's regional rules).
Your date of birth itself is never transmitted. It is read on your device and stored only on your device. Only the age classification derived from it is included in advertising and consent requests sent to Google. No account or contact details are ever attached to it.

The app does not mark its advertising as child-directed, because it does not permit use by anyone under 13.

5. Advertisements, Personalization & Consent

The app displays personalized advertisements through Google AdMob, using banner and interstitial ad formats. Advertisers and their partners may collect or receive:

  • Advertising identifiers (for example, Android Advertising ID)
  • Ad interaction data (ad views, clicks)
  • Device information (OS version, device model)
  • IP address and approximate location (used for ad delivery and fraud prevention)
  • Your age classification, as described in section 4

EU/EEA/UK users — UMP SDK

For users in the EU/EEA and the UK, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to present a consent form and record your choice for ad personalization.

  • The consent form lets you accept ads, reject them, or manage your choices vendor by vendor.
  • Your choice is respected and applied to ad requests.
  • You can change your decision at any time by reopening the "Consent Form" option in the app's Options screen. This option is available wherever Google's rules require it.
  • Ads are personalized by default, but if those are not possible due to an error, the app will display non-personalized ads.
  • If you explicitly decline ad storage/consent required by some ad providers, any kind of ads will not show at all.
  • If you are under the age of digital consent, the app signals that to Google so that age-restricted advertising rules can be applied.
Note: ad networks have their own data practices. For details and global ad settings you can review Google's Ads Settings and AdMob documentation. You can also control or reset your advertising identifier through your device's system settings.

6. Third-Party Services & Content Sources

Services that process data at runtime

  • Google AdMob — used to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and process data as described in section 5. See Google's privacy documentation for details.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) — used to present the consent form and relay your advertising consent choice.

Tools used to produce the bundled content

The learning content shipped inside the app — Japanese vocabulary, definitions, example sentences and pronunciation audio — was prepared offline by the developer before release. It is bundled inside the app and served entirely from your device.

  • Definitions and example sentences were created with the assistance of AI tools.
  • Pronunciation audio included with the app was generated using LuvVoice, TTSMP3 and TTSMaker.
These tools are not used at runtime. The app makes no network requests to LuvVoice, TTSMP3, TTSMaker, or any AI service. The text and audio they helped produce was the app's own learning content, not your personal data, and none of your activity, study data, or device data is sent to them.

7. Data Security

  • Your date of birth, language preferences, practice progress, and consent choices are stored in the app's private storage on your device. They are not included in device backups, and other apps cannot read them.
  • We do not receive personal data from you, and we operate no server that could be compromised.
  • Advertising and consent requests are made over encrypted connections to Google.
  • Development and testing helpers, including a debug-only consent testing tool, are removed from release builds of the app.

8. Data Retention

  • Local app data — including your date of birth, preferences and study progress — remains on your device until you clear the app's storage, uninstall the app, or reset it from your device settings.
  • Advertising and consent data held by Google AdMob and its partners is retained according to their own retention policies. Please review those services' privacy policies for details.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location you may have rights under applicable privacy laws (for example, GDPR in the EU/EEA/UK) including the rights to:

  • access personal data;
  • request correction or deletion;
  • restrict or object to certain processing (including ad personalization); and

How to exercise these rights in practice. Because we receive no personal data, we cannot retrieve, correct, or delete a copy for you — none exists. To change or withdraw advertising consent, reopen the "Consent Form" option in the app's Options screen. To control advertising more broadly, use your device settings and Google's Ads Settings.

For anything else, including questions about this policy, contact us at the address below. We will respond in accordance with applicable law.

10. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed to children. The app has a minimum age of 13: a date of birth is requested at onboarding, and users below that age are not permitted to proceed. The app does not serve child-directed advertising and does not enable advertising personalization for users it has determined to be under the age of digital consent.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps to remove that information.

The tools listed in section 6 were used offline to produce bundled content and do not receive or process your personal data. For details of Google's international transfer safeguards, consult Google's privacy documentation or contact us.
